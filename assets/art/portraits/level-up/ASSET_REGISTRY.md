# Level-up Portrait Asset Registry

| UI role | Position | Asset | Recommended source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Necro Queen | LEFT / wallpaper | `assets/art/portraits/level-up/necro-queen-level-up.png` | 768×1024 or larger, portrait |

The level-up portrait is intentionally different from the circular dialogue icon.
It should be a tall atmospheric portrait with the Queen weighted toward the left/centre and dark negative space toward the right so the level information and trait choices can sit above it without becoming visually confused.
