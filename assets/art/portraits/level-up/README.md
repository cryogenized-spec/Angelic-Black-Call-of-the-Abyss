# Level-up portrait

Canonical full-height portrait/wallpaper for the level-up book.

Upload:
- `necro-queen-level-up.png`

Recommended source artwork:
- Portrait orientation.
- Prefer 768×1024 or larger.
- Show the Necro Queen from head through at least mid-thigh/full torso.
- Compose her toward the left/centre of the image so the right side can disappear naturally into dark negative space.
- Strong silhouette and atmospheric fade are preferred over a busy background.

Runtime placement:
- The portrait occupies the left side of the level-up panel as a visual wallpaper.
- Level number, trait information and selectable options occupy the right side.
- The portrait should remain subordinate to the readable options rather than competing with them.
