# Dialogue portraits

Canonical conversational portrait assets.

The Necro Queen's portrait is rendered as a circular/badge portrait on the **left** side of the dialogue panel.
Other speaking characters are rendered as circular/badge portraits on the **right** side.

Recommended source artwork:
- `necro-queen-dialogue.png` — 1:1 square, 512×512 or larger, centered bust/face, transparent or dark neutral background.
- `name-a-dialogue.png` — 1:1 square.
- `name-b-dialogue.png` — 1:1 square.
- `witch-dialogue.png` — 1:1 square.
- `mara-dialogue.png` — 1:1 square.
- `nobleman-dialogue.png` — 1:1 square.
- `jester-dialogue.png` — 1:1 square.

Do not crop the Queen's portrait as a full-body image. This asset is specifically a conversational badge.
