### Format

Preferred source size: 512×512 or larger, square 1:1.
Use a clean bust/head composition with the face large enough to remain readable inside a circular crop.
Keep the Queen's icon visually distinct from her level-up wallpaper portrait.

### Dialogue layout rule

The Necro Queen always occupies the **left** portrait position.
Every other speaking character occupies the **right** portrait position.
Narration / stage directions use no portrait.
